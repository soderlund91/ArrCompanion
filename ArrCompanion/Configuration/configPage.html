Arr Companion v0.2.0.0

The titles Home Screen Companion finds missing from the library, and where they are on its lists. Add them to Sonarr and Radarr by hand, or let rules add them.

A rule adds the titles that are missing and high on a list, for example the top 3 of a trending list. A title a rule has added once is not added again, even if it is removed from Sonarr or Radarr later. Rules use the root folder and quality profile set under Settings.
They also run on the scheduled task Arr Companion › Run rules (every 6 hours unless changed under Scheduled Tasks).
